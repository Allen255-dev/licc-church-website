Our story, our family.

Living Waters International Christian Center exists to introduce people to Jesus — the source of living water — and help them grow into who He's called them to be.

Our Mission

We enthrone men as friends of God

We exist to lift every person into a place of honor and intimacy with God, because we believe that humanity's highest purpose is not servitude, but genuine friendship with our Creator.

Our Vision

Unveiling God's faithfulness and power in His name

We are committed to consistently revealing and demonstrating that God's character is utterly trustworthy and that His power is real, active, and available today — because every display of His faithfulness and might brings glory to His name and transforms lives.

Our Story

How we began

Living Waters International Christian Center was founded by Rev. Francis Kpeke-Johnson, who carried a vision to plant a church in Port Harcourt rooted in the Word of God and full of life through the Holy Spirit.

From that founding vision, the church has grown into a family of believers gathering at our home along the Ahoada East–West Road in Rukpakwulosi — a place where newcomers and long-time members alike find a church to call home.

What We Believe

A brief peek into the LICC commission

Here in LICC, we "SLIG" it — that is, we SERVE, LINK, INVITE and GIVE.

S stands for SERVE

Serve God with your gift in a unit in the LICC.

This means that every unique talent, skill, or ability you have is not accidental — it is a divine deposit meant to be used for God's glory.

L stands for LINK

Link up with a pastoral care fellowship in the LICC.

This means that you are not meant to walk the Christian journey alone — connection is essential for growth, support, and accountability.

I stands for INVITE

Invite one person to church every time you are coming for service in the LICC.

This means that evangelism is not left to pastors or a select few — it is a lifestyle for every believer.

G stands for GIVE

Give to advance the work of God in the LICC by your offerings, tithes, and partnership.

This means that giving is not just a religious duty — it is a powerful act of worship and a tangible way to partner with God in building His kingdom.

The LICC Faith Confession

What we declare together

I am a friend of God, a citizen of His presence. I enjoy God's faithfulness and power. I enthrone nations as friends of God. The high praise of God is in my mouth, and a two-edged sword in my hands, as I exercise dominion on the face of the earth.

I am a friend of God, a citizen of His presence. I believe in Jesus Christ as my Lord and Savior. I am seated in Christ Jesus far above principalities and powers. I am above only and never beneath. I am the head and not the tail. The hand of God is upon my life for speed, restoration and favour.

I am a friend of God, a citizen of His presence. I love, listen to, and live by the Word of God. I am praiseful, prayerful, and there is power in my prophecy! I know who I am — I am a royal priesthood, a chosen generation, an ambassador of Yewah.

I am a friend of God, a citizen of His presence. Out of my belly flows the river of living water. I am life everywhere I go. I am a voice that can't be silenced. I am a light that can't be quenched. I am a glory that compels. I am a fire that burns.

I am a friend of God, a citizen of His presence. I am wise, powerful, and influential! Wealth and riches are with me. I lend to nations, I don't borrow. Nothing dies in my hands, and I prosper in all things, because I am helped by God.

In this year 2026, I recover all the enemy stole from me by the power of Jesus Christ, because Jesus came to restore life in abundance to me. I take back my inheritance in Christ: power, riches, wisdom, strength, honour, glory and blessings.

The prophecy of the month is at work in my life. Evil assignments are removed far away from my family and I. All the devices of the crafty are disappointed and can't touch me, because I bear on my body the mark of Jesus; therefore no man can trouble my life.

Every covenant in the LICC commission is speaking for me. I am connected to the grace resident here. I enjoy the covenant of divine health, long life and prosperity. And the lines are falling to me in pleasant places, because I am a friend of God and a citizen of His presence!
